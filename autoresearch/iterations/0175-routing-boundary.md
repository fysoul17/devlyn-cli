## Selection decision and evidence

Bare sufficed on the inspected L/C contracts: local standard-library behavior or
a bounded in-memory dependency graph with a decisive oracle. Observed full-route
native times were5.77–8.61 times bare, with no demonstrated quality lift there.
This does not override explicit spec/resolve/queue instructions or executor pins.

For H, independent primary review found real ownership defects in both full draws.
Pair repaired the measured precision failure; solo's partial repair exhausted the
canonical verify budget and correctly stopped. The difference is worth studying,
but both findings originated with Codex and the first pair Opus missed the defect.
OTHER-engine incremental value is therefore unproved. All three H products also
missed the unbounded finite-int requirement, so none meets the whole quality floor.

Proposed rule for confirmation: inspect representation boundaries, durable ownership,
and oracle gaps when deciding whether independent verification earns its cost;
do not use module count or a generic difficulty score. Keep existing risk and
explicit-workflow obligations. Pair preference needs an incremental result beyond
solo on multiple untouched tasks/repeated draws with a prospectively registered
sample and resource ceiling. That confirmation is not started here: this nine-draw
screen cannot establish a production threshold. No classifier, engine layer,
installer/skill change, extra task draw, or npm release.
