## What counts as improvement

The seven principles, cleanup of orphans created by the change, user scope,
executor pin and delivery policy remain intact. An instruction change is a
hypothesis, not a quality guarantee. Compare the same engine and settings in:

- A: its native CLI without devlyn;

- C: the same package with one instruction change.

Record correct completions and concrete contract violations first. Charge all
attempts, including failures and native children, to total owner wall time,
input and output per correct completion. Zero successes have no finite
per-success cost. Missing telemetry is unknown and cannot support admission.
Independent experimental assessment is recorded separately; if assessment or
retry becomes part of the product mechanism, its full cost belongs to that
mechanism. A candidate must improve quality or a resource measure without
regressing quality, wall, input or output per correct completion, for each
engine on which it would ship. Aggregating engines cannot conceal regression.

Process observations explain results; fewer calls, more stages or longer
reasoning are not themselves successful outcomes. Measured improvement on a
small panel is a scoped result, never proof of a global capability maximum.
