## Decision

Keep direct work with proportionate actual checks and independent review for our
work. The evidence supports fresh review connected to repair in specific cases;
it does not establish that the complete fixed phase graph is necessary to obtain
that benefit. It also does not establish that bare prompting always suffices.
Stop spending the next unit on another fully specified one-line native/minimal
comparison. Do not add another reminder, build a replacement framework, or remove
production safeguards from this audit. Existing customer routing remains unchanged.

**Named evidence delta from 0195:** that study compared native with minimal
instructions, not actual resolve. This audit additionally checks historical actual
full runs, including the contrary hard-task result in0185. Thus its tie alone is
not the reason to retain or retire resolve. Product fixes to the harness, such as
0195's classifier repair, establish integrity of that component, not added value
on a user's development task.

## Next bounded question under the no-resolve constraint

Test whether a fresh reviewer adds request-completion value beyond **equally
budgeted additional self-review and repair**, without the full phase graph.
0184 already observed fresh same-model review/repair gains and compared reviewer
models, but not fresh context against continued self-review. Its coded cwd also
limited blinding. In contrast,
0186 mixed focus, context, tools and phases.0185's self-verifying B versus full
C also differed in phases and budget. None isolates this question.

Use unchanged direct implementation, seal each initial product, then compare
continued self-review with fresh same-model review. Both reviews are read-only;
both findings packets go to identically configured fresh repair workers, including
empty-finding branches. Fix equal per-stage wall-time ceilings, model/effort,
call caps, visible source/request/checks and scope before dispatch; prior reviewer
context is the intended difference, not repairer context. Exact limits belong in
registration, including one arm-blind findings schema and arm-blind scoring. Score final
requirements, correct closures, false findings and repair harm before total time.
Do not add the unproven0190 reminder or make OTHER-model identity another factor.
Peer Fable/Grok advice remains separate from experimental treatment/scoring.

This is an admission design, **not a frozen protocol or a launched experiment**:
an eligible natural task, acceptance oracle, exact replayable continuation
transport and holdout are not yet registered. Select tasks prospectively by real
request and requirement interaction, never by bare-arm failure. Run every branch,
including initially correct products; apply discovered counterexamples uniformly
and label post-exposure probes. Do not tune on confirmation tasks. Reject promotion
on scope/requirement regressions; ties supply no incremental quality evidence.
If admission cannot be satisfied, leave this research question pending instead
of manufacturing another saturated repair. No external developer was contacted.

Such a study can measure a direct-review mechanism only. It cannot certify a
replacement for resolve, default paired quality, or gate15. Those remain open;
current no-resolve instructions are not grounds to rewrite their definitions.
