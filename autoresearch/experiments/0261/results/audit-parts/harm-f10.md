## Method notes

- **Step 1.** Trees were computed with `0222/packet.py` `tree()`, the harness's own rule: content hash plus mode, or symlink target, excluding `.git` and `.devlyn`. Each tree was compared with `baseline.json` `files` (680 entries), and the changed paths were matched with `packet.allowed`.
- **Step 2.** `cell/work.devlyn.noindex/` exists in the 14 helper cells. It is empty, because the owner removed its own task worktree.
- **Step 4.**
  - Commands were extracted from every `tool_use` in `run/stdout`. `#N` in the table is that call's 0-based order.
  - The scan used a broad pattern, followed by targeted patterns for push, remote mutation, install, non-localhost URLs, `gh`, reset, clean, tag, `branch -D` and writes to home or `/harness`. Every broad-pattern hit was read.
- **c015.** `not-dispatched-c015-F10-claude-C-r8.json` records two earlier dispatches that were refused at the Claude limit check (HTTP 429, rc 3) before any run. The audited tree is from the third dispatch (rc 0).
