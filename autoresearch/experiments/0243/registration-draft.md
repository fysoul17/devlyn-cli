## Evidence and decisions

Correct completion requires all public checks, all four frozen oracle rows,
allowed source scope, and a local commit containing exactly the evaluated
source. Record automatic activation, peer completion, novel findings, owner
reproduction and repair independently. No activation does not make otherwise
correct source incorrect by definition; it supplies no independent-check credit.
Failed attempts followed by valid recovery retain all costs without permanently
failing an otherwise correct result. Stable-source and peer ancestry rules hold.

Record total owner wall, input including cache and output for every attempt and
native child. Count reported thinking once. Unknown usage is never zero; retain
known lower bounds and stop on an identity/accounting/evaluator machinery fault.
A product failure remains a measured failure. Stop on authentication faults.
No favorable reroll, oracle adjustment or post-result gate relaxation. No
independent model call, reviewer or heavy test overlaps a measured owner.

Judge each engine separately, with A reported alongside. A screen signal is more
correct completions or equal correctness with lower resources. Reject a treatment
that gives no quality/resource benefit over B, or raises resources per correct
completion without a quality benefit. H must justify independence against S;
P must justify its route against S and H. With zero successes, per-correct cost
is undefined; report total failed-run costs without manufacturing a finite ratio.

A signal requires separately registered harder ownership/recovery cases, fresh
repeated confirmation and easy/orphan controls. Final admission preserves0237's
per-engine quality/wall/input/output non-regression rules against the strongest
baseline. No signal closes this mechanism. Neither outcome establishes a global
solo/pair ceiling, and no bare-model superiority claim is licensed without it.

Keep the pinned current-CLI image, --init/PID256/memory4g/CPU2, owner5400,
review600/evaluator600/peer540 watchdogs and supported6300-second authentication
margin. No token, cost or call budgets, credential edits, background-wait changes
or extra intervention to force activation.
