## Powered study, design round 1

**Question.** Separately for claude and for codex, on 0233's tasks with 0256's pins, routes, image, and grading, does published `devlyn-cli@4.2.24` (B) meet 0232 §6 against no installation (A) when replicates are pooled per task? Engines are never pooled. The estimand is that admission decision. Completion's estimand is the per-task difference in completion rate. The package is one confounded object. I0185 stays in the package estimand and out of any principles-text claim.

**Panels and n.** No new tasks. More replicates test whether 0256's contrast is stable on these fixtures. They leave unseen tasks, the split among `task-completion.md`, `task-complete.py`, the installer, and `devlyn-ideate`, and any principles effect on I0185 unmeasured. New tasks would be a second population in the same run.

Gating development: D3, D4, I0185, B5. Confirmation, reported and not gating: F10, F11 at the same n, because the secondary endpoint lives there. Easy E1 and E2: one replicate, a tripwire, reported. **n = 10** per (task, engine, arm) on development and confirmation. Measured cells: 4×2×2×10 + 2×2×2×10 + 2×2×2×1 = **248**, plus 4 SMOKE outside every sum.

**Completion power.** One-sided Fisher exact, α = 0.05, per task. At n = 10, 10 vs 7 does not reject (p ≈ 0.10) and 10 vs 6 does (p ≈ 0.043); 7 vs 5 does not (p ≈ 0.33). A one- or two-cell dip stays inconclusive. A boundary gap of the size 0256 treated as decisive (1.0 vs 0.5, or 0.5 vs 0.0) falls in the reject tail with probability about 0.8 when the better arm is truly at 0 or 1. An interior 0.75 vs 0.25 gap is about 80% by the two-proportion formula. Astra's ~14 was the 90% figure and does not fit this account without a third week of holds. Saturated tasks add no false rejects, so the four-task family is not Bonferroni-adjusted. If both rates sit inside (0, 1), power is lower than the boundary figure.

**Primary rule,** per engine, development, first match. (1) **NOT_FINISHED:** a slot lacks a verdict, an unresolved STOP, a mandatory judgment, or a passed instruction assertion. (2) **NO_CLAIM:** B has zero panel successes. (3) **PACKAGE_LOWERS_QUALITY:** a task's completion Fisher rejects, a check or oracle row's Fisher rejects, or the witness union below fails. (4) **PACKAGE_UNSAFE:** a B cell has a false completion, a scope violation, or user-data harm. One cell is enough. Fisher does not apply, and A's events do not offset B. (5) **INCONCLUSIVE:** a completion or row count is lower and Fisher does not reject, with (3) and (4) clear. (6) **PACKAGE_COSTS_MORE.** (7) **COST_UNKNOWN.** (8) **NO_STRICT_GAIN:** pooled quality counts hold, safety is clean, and nothing strictly improves. That is the no-failure-observed outcome. Admission fails. (9) **DEVELOPMENT_PASS.** Confirmation and easy use the same rule and never gate. 0256 accounting is unchanged and is applied only once quality holds. K7 stays binding.

The severe gate is Grok's: per task and engine, the witnesses that reproduce on B are a subset of those that reproduce on A. Counts and per-replicate deltas are published and do not gate. Hard counts at n = 10 fail common witnesses under equal rates, and 0256's codex I0185 set already fires on both arms, so the panel would read `PACKAGE_LOWERS_QUALITY` by arithmetic. The union matches the addendum's pairing failure: a one-and-one swap passes. Its cost, a higher B rate of a witness A shows once, stays visible for a later registration.

**Secondary.** Astra's, and not a gate. On this study's fresh F10/F11 cells only, a frozen witness POSTs malformed JSON and passes when the response is not Express's default HTML 400. It does not enter the token and licenses no text edit.

**Schedule and stopping.** Freeze the prediction, the rule, the accounting, and this stopping rule before SMOKE. Order: SMOKE, the easy tripwire, then replicates 1..10 of D3, D4, I0185, B5, F10, F11. Arms of a (task, engine) block run back to back, AB/BA as in 0256, reversed on the next replicate. One cell at a time. Caps are seven-day 85% and five-hour 70%. Measured Claude-config cells are 124 × ~0.5 point ≈ 62 points from the current ~23%, so the run crosses 85% and waits for the seven-day reset. The five-hour window holds the same way. A hold is not a verdict. User-data harm stops dispatch. The token stays **NOT_FINISHED**, and the harm is published as an absolute event. Any other stop, including a finished replicate prefix, is **NOT_FINISHED**. Fisher is not read on a prefix.
