I implemented `slugify` and added the regression tests. Both are merged into `main` through [PR #1](https://github.com/fysoul17/rpd7-20261018a-smoke-claude/pull/1), and the public check passes.

**What I changed** (only the two allowed files):
- **`src/slugtools/__init__.py`**: `slugify` now raises `TypeError` for anything that isn't a string. Otherwise it replaces each run of characters outside `A–Z`, `a–z` and `0–9` with one hyphen, strips leading and trailing hyphens, and lowercases the result. Empty or separator-only input returns `""`.
  - It lowercases *after* replacing characters on purpose. Python's `str.lower()` turns a few non-ASCII characters into ASCII letters (the Kelvin sign `K` becomes `k`), so lowercasing first would let them slip into the slug. A comment in the code explains this.
- **`tests/test_regression.py`** (new): covers punctuation and spaces, repeated separators, leading and trailing separators, digits, accented letters (`"Café Ünïcode"` → `"caf-n-code"`), the Kelvin sign, empty and separator-only input, and `TypeError` for `None`, an int, bytes and a list.
