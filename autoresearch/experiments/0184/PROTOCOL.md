# 0184 — peer review connected to repair

## Why this exists

Mission 1, layer-cost-justified decision: should bounded direct development always
pay for OTHER-model review plus repair, or is an equally budgeted fresh same-model
review/repair sufficient? The owner explicitly authorized this next comparison.
Root implements the research directly. No resolve run is needed in this experiment.
Keep difficult, multi-module/state/concurrency work as a separate later comparison;
this small filesystem-tool study is not evidence to bypass its full safeguards.

## Prediction and judgment

Prediction: OTHER review repairs more missed requirements than same-model review,
without increasing complete-to-incomplete regressions. If initial products all pass,
there is no observed failure-reduction headroom; still finish the registered branches
and measure preservation, false positives and added cost. No mandatory peer-review
promotion without replicated net quality benefit that justifies cost.

Freeze requests, source, checks, candidate, runner, prompts and native settings.
Calibrate actual-CLI checks with original/reference/mutant controls before dispatch.
Keep external checks out of every participant. Seal all final products before external
assessment of initial and repaired sources. Score completion first, incomplete→complete
repairs and complete→incomplete harm, then generation+review+repair native wall time,
external assessment time and native counters. Count generation once in actual spend,
